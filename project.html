Case Study

TxDOT Maritime Division: Operational Tracking & Analysis

Texas Department of Transportation • Business Operations

Organization: TxDOT Maritime Division
Role: Intern Support Technician
Focus Area: Business Operations & Data Tracking
Key Tools: Microsoft Excel • Pivot Tables

1. Project Overview

During my internship with the Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT) Maritime Division within Business Operations, I led an operational tracking project to centralize and analyze division activities.

The project focused on compiling and structuring information from diverse operational sources—including financial documents, travel expenses, and special projects—into a cohesive tracking dashboard. Additionally, I conducted an in-depth analysis of 12 months of operational and financial data to identify recurring patterns and support informed business decisions.

2. The Problem

Operational information within the division was distributed across different administrative channels, financial records, and project files. As a result, tracking operational activity on an ongoing basis required manual compilation and was difficult to review efficiently.

The division needed an organized, centralized structure so team members and leadership could track operational activity, monitor ongoing workflows, and review progress more easily.

3. My Contribution

To address these challenges, I took ownership of structuring and standardizing the division's operational data:

  • Information Organization: Gathered, cleaned, and organized records from financial documents, staff travel expenses, and division-wide special projects into an accessible tracking dashboard.
  • Performance Metric Tracking: Structured the tracking system to monitor key operational indicators, including reimbursement processing time and milestone progression for special projects.
  • Data Analysis: Evaluated 12 months of historical operational and financial data to highlight trends, observe resource allocations, and provide clear summaries for operational leadership.

4. Tools Used

The solution was built using core analytical tools accessible across the division:

Microsoft Excel

Used for data consolidation, structuring tabular datasets, organizing line-item expenses, and standardizing record formats.

Excel Pivot Tables

Leveraged to summarize large datasets, segment expense categories, calculate turnaround timelines, and generate high-level operational summaries.

5. Value Provided

The operational tracking dashboard and data analysis provided tangible operational value for the Maritime Division:

  • Better Operational Visibility: Centralized disparate administrative records into a single dashboard, enabling staff and managers to review division activities at a glance.
  • Support for Business Decisions: Provided division leadership with clear 12-month historical trends to evaluate workflows and make data-backed operational decisions.
  • Process Monitoring: Created visibility into reimbursement processing times, making it simpler to track timelines and identify potential delays.
  • Project Oversight: Offered a structured view into the status and timeline of special projects underway within the division.

6. What I Learned

This experience strengthened my understanding of business operations and public-sector data management:

  • Data Structuring: Developing clean, well-categorized datasets from fragmented sources is essential before any meaningful analysis or dashboarding can occur.
  • Practical Analysis: Pivot tables and focused spreadsheet modeling can provide powerful, immediate clarity without requiring overly complex software infrastructure.
  • Business Impact: Effective operational tracking directly supports organizational accountability, faster processing times, and better-informed decision-making.
